AtomX Pay · V6 · motion lab

One page, four physics

Each experiment is the V1 warm flagship, untouched in layout and copy, with one family of motion borrowed from the Componentry library bolted on. Same page, four different ways it can feel alive.

All effects are hand-built vanilla JS and CSS (no React, no Framer Motion), gated behind prefers-reduced-motion and desktop hover so touch and reduced-motion users get the calm base. Open each and move your cursor, type in the calculator, and scroll.

Version 6a

Magnetic pull

Interactive elements are physically attracted to the cursor. The closer you get, the more they lean in.

Where to feel it: hover near the CTA buttons and nav links (they slide toward you, arrow pulls further), then sweep across the dark ticker (ticks swell and glow as they pass).

Open 6a → Version 6b

Spring physics

Everything moves with elastic, slightly-overshooting springs instead of rigid tracking. It bounces, then settles.

Where to feel it: tilt the hero photo cards with your cursor (they overshoot and wobble back), type in the calculator (the received amount pops), and scroll cards into view (they spring past, then settle).

Open 6b → Version 6c

Festival particles

A restrained marigold particle system, tuned for Diwali warmth rather than generic confetti.

Where to feel it: type a higher amount in the calculator (a gold petal burst rises from the received figure), watch the slow ambient petals drift behind the hero, and catch the ticker stars twinkle.

Open 6c → Version 6d

Kinetic scroll

The page responds to how fast you scroll, and dark bands light up under your cursor.

Where to feel it: scroll quickly (the ticker speeds up and skews with your velocity), then hover the dark ticker and final CTA (a warm spotlight follows the cursor).
