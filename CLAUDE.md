# AtomX Pay — Project Instructions

## Source of truth for all content

**Always reference [`ATOMX_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md`](./ATOMX_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md) before writing or editing any website, page, or variant.** It is the canonical knowledge base extracted from the live AtomX Pay properties:

- `https://www.atomxpay.com/` — public marketing site
- `https://sandbox.atomxpay.com/` and `https://sandbox.atomxpay.com/v1` — product app

Rules:
- Every site we build (any version/variant) must use the facts, copy, numbers, fees, contact details, and compliance language from the knowledge base. Do not invent fees, claims, stats, or legal entities that are not grounded there.
- If the source sites change, re-extract and update `ATOMX_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md` (the sites are client-rendered SPAs — content lives in the JS bundles, not the static HTML).
- Canonical numbers: 15-minute settlement, $4 flat fee, 0% FX markup, min $2, USD→INR corridor, MSB-licensed (US) / FIU-registered (India).

## Copy style
- Bright, warm, emotional, family-oriented remittance tone.
- Do not use em dashes in generated copy.
