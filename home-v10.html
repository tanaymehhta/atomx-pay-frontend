GOOD QUESTIONS

Everything you might ask.

How long does a transfer really take?+

Most transfers land in the recipient’s Indian bank in about 15 minutes, any day of the year. No business-day waiting.

What rate and fees do you use?+

The live mid-market rate with no spread added and a $4 flat fee. What the calculator shows is what your family receives (first transfer is free).

Are you regulated?+

Yes. AtomX Pay Inc is a FinCEN-registered MSB in the United States. INR payouts are executed through FIU-IND registered partners in India. Every transfer is screened against AML and CFT lists.

What documents do I need?+

A government-issued photo ID and a quick selfie for identity verification. This is a one-time step required by US federal regulations.
